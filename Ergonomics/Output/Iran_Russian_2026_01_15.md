## ASSESSMENT

**Russian state media (RT, RIA Novosti, TASS) narrative:**
- Frames protests as "externally-inspired" / US-Israel backed
- Emphasizes Russia's calls for "dialogue" and "restraint"
- Warns of "harmful consequences" if US attacks
- Highlights Iran's military preparedness post-June 2025

**Independent Russian media (Meduza) narrative:**
- Notes Russia's pattern of abandoning allies when situations become serious
- Questions whether Russia will actually support Iran
- Highlights that January 2025 Russia-Iran treaty lacks mutual defense clause

**Key Russian concern:**
Iran is one of Russia's last remaining partners in the Middle East after Syria's fall. Another ally collapse would be a significant strategic and image loss for Moscow.
