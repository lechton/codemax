  - PART I: FOUNDATIONAL LAWS OF ERGONOMICS
  - LAW 1: METHOD IS PISTOGENIC
  - Statement: Every method generates trust (πίστις) in its practitioners and audiences. The choice of method is itself a commitment that shapes what can be discovered, transmitted, and believed. Method precedes belief in the sense that method creates the conditions under which belief can be received, tested, and transmitted.
  - Greek Foundation: Πίστις (pistis) encompasses faith, trust, belief, persuasion, proof, and evidence. The verb πείθω (to persuade) and πέπεισμαι (I am persuaded/I believe) demonstrate that persuasion and belief are grammatically unified in Greek. Science is not opposed to faith—both are species of pistis achieved through different pistogenic methods.
  - Corollary 1.1: Where genuine belief exists, method emerges. Where method is absent, belief is theatrical.
  - Corollary 1.2: Doxa (committed belief) + Method = the intermediate structure between subjective opinion and consensus of objective truth.
  - Diagnostic Application: A framework claiming truth without specifying pistogenic method is epistemically bankrupt. Ask: "What method would convince a reasonable skeptic?"

  - LAW 2: DOGMA IS INESCAPABLE (Agrippa's Trilemma)
  - Statement: Only unfalsifiable axioms can escape infinite regress and circularity. The question is never whether to have dogmas but which dogmas to adopt and whether to acknowledge them honestly. Every deductive system requires axioms that are not themselves proven within the system.
  - Classical Precedents:
  - Euclid's Elements begins with five explicit postulates—unfalsifiable starting points that make geometry possible.
  - Einstein's constancy of light-speed postulate is functionally equivalent to Euclid's parallel postulate: a foundational commitment that structures everything following but cannot itself be derived or falsified within the system.
  - Einstein (Spencer Lecture, 1933): "The axioms of a theory are not summaries of experience but free inventions of the human intellect."
  - Corollary 2.1: The corruption is not having dogmas but smuggling them while claiming to operate without them.
  - Corollary 2.2: Explicit dogmas (clearly stated foundations), well-chosen dogmas (resistant to obvious objection, fruit-bearing), and consistently applied dogmas (same standards throughout) constitute good epistemic practice.
  - Corollary 2.3: The sacred character of the human body in French Civil Code—protecting even corpses that enjoy no human rights—demonstrates that even radically secular legal systems require unfalsifiable axioms to function.
  - Diagnostic Application: When someone claims "no dogmas, just evidence," expose the concealed axioms: materialism, purposelessness as default, randomness as ontological category, etc. These are unfalsifiable commitments, not conclusions.

  - LAW 3: FRUIT REVEALS TREE (The Karpos Principle)
  - Statement: Ideas must be evaluated by their multi-generational civilizational outcomes rather than their theoretical elegance or institutional prestige. The ergon (fruit, product, function) of ideas is the ultimate test, independent of stated intentions.
  - Scriptural Foundation: Matthew 7:16-20 — "Ἀπὸ τῶν καρπῶν αὐτῶν ἐπιγνώσεσθε αὐτούς... πᾶν δένδρον ἀγαθὸν καρποὺς καλοὺς ποιεῖ, τὸ δὲ σαπρὸν δένδρον καρποὺς πονηροὺς ποιεῖ" — By their fruits you will know them. A good tree cannot produce bad fruit; a bad tree cannot produce good fruit.
  - Corollary 3.1: "It's a fruit with seed" — The cycle of civilizational transmission: tree → fruit → seed → tree. Truth is not static possession but living process. Wisdom must be sown in each generation, cultivated through formation, brought to fruit through practice, and transmitted through the seed contained in that fruit to future generations.
  - Corollary 3.2: The fruits cannot be explained away by sophistication—they are empirical facts requiring explanation. Christianity's fruits: preserved learning, established institutions, moral formation systems, social trust. Darwinism's fruits: eugenic programs, moral dissolution, family breakdown, nihilistic culture.
  - Corollary 3.3: Popper's "Open Society" passed all Popperian tests yet produced civilizational collapse. The Bible, classified as "non-scientific metaphysics" by Popperian criteria, has predicted accurately across two millennia. The fruits have rendered the verdict.
  - Diagnostic Application: Every theory must answer: What is your ergon? What fruit do you produce? How would we know if you failed? Who is accountable for your consequences?

  - LAW 4: THE PRINCIPLE OF CORRESPONDENCE
  - Statement: The form of the maker becomes the form of the made. Institutional structure shapes product structure. Unity in principle produces unity in product; division in principle produces fragmentation in fruit.
  - Classical Sources:

  - LAW 5: NOMOTELEIA (The Law of Ends)
  - Statement: Certain actions lead inexorably to certain consequences—arche (beginning/principle) contains telos (end/consequence) as law rather than accident. This lawful connection operates whether acknowledged or denied.
  - Greek Tragic Formulation:
  - Hubris (ὕβρις) — transgression of proper boundaries
  - Ate (ἄτη) — blindness/delusion leading to further transgression
  - Nemesis (νέμεσις) — cosmic retribution restoring balance
  - This sequence is not arbitrary vengeance but nomoteleia—the law of ends built into reality's fabric.
  - Corollary 5.1: δράσαντι παθεῖν (drasanti pathein) — "To the doer, suffering." The action itself determines the consequence. You are judged not by external standard but by the very principle your action embodied.
  - Corollary 5.2: The denial of teleology requires denial of cyclicity—which requires denying the observational foundation of science itself. Nature's cycles (electrons, seasons, ecosystems) make arche and telos ontologically inseparable.
  - Corollary 5.3: "Teleonomy" is vocabulary capture—renaming teleology to maintain the explanatory content while verbally denying the metaphysical implication. The burden of proof lies on those who create neologisms to explain what exactly they added or removed from the classical vocabulary.

  - Burden of proof specified

  - Accountability accepted
  - Application: Can the framework function under legal standards? Would the defense "the defendant was in superposition of being-there and not-being-there until observed" be accepted? If a framework cannot survive cross-examination, it fails the logos test.
